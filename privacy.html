In short: TripArchive collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data storage

All trips, locations, settings, and backups are stored locally on your device. The app has no server, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Nothing you enter is ever transmitted to us or to any third party by the app.

Data leaving your device

Data leaves your device only when you explicitly choose to export or share it — for example exporting an Excel, PDF, or CSV report, sharing a backup file, or sharing the diagnostics summary. In every case the Android share dialog opens and you choose the destination yourself.

Permissions

TripArchive requests no permissions at all. It has no location, camera, contacts, storage, or Internet access — the app cannot connect to a network under any circumstance.

Device backups

If you use Android's built-in backup or device-to-device transfer, your TripArchive data is included so it can move to your new phone. That process is operated by Android under your Google account settings, not by us.

Because all of your data is stored only on your device, keeping it safe is your responsibility. If your device is lost, stolen, damaged, reset to factory settings, or if the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared, your on-device data may be permanently lost and cannot be recovered by the developer. You are solely responsible for regularly backing up and exporting your trip history using the app's built-in Backup and Export features. The app does not provide tax, accounting, or legal advice, and exported reports do not constitute certified records.
